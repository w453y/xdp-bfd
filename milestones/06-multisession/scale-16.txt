Scale validation at 16 concurrent sessions (2 peer VMs x 8 addresses).

L3 stress 120s, 16 sessions: 16 up before and after, all 16
down-event counters 0. Per-slot TX gaps (host pcap, full window
including the mass-kill below):
  surviving slots 49152,49154-49160: p50 ~8.75ms p99 ~10.02ms
  max 10.5-12.1ms
  killed slots 49161-49167 (+49153, see note): max ~1003ms = the
  1s slow-rate heartbeat during peer death, not a stress artifact.

Mass kill: pkill -9 bfdd on the 8-session peer. All 8 detected:
  30.5 32.7 31.3 31.4 31.9 32.8 30.8 32.6 ms silent
No drift across the batch - the 5ms sweep does not serialize
detectably at this N. The other 8 sessions: uptimes uninterrupted,
down events stayed 0. Note: slot 49153 (.3, killed peer's primary)
flapped once more during that peer's frr restart (peer-side
settling), hence its 1002ms max.

FRR operational findings (10.5.1), for reproduction:
- bfdd peer identity is (peer addr, ifname, vrf); local-address is
  an attribute, not part of the key. Configuring the same peer addr
  with different local-addresses edits one node, it does not create
  sessions. N sessions between two hosts need N distinct addresses
  on BOTH ends.
- Changing local-address on an existing peer node does not
  propagate to the dataplane: bfdd keeps running the old addressing
  (verified on the wire). Recreate the peer, or write memory +
  restart frr so fresh ADDs carry the local address.
