Demand-mode verification poll, both arms, against stock FRR bfdd.
One mesh session (10.66.0.115 -> 10.66.0.15) with demand-mode configured
on the DUT side only, so the DUT's detection timer is held while the peer,
having been asked to, stops transmitting. The DUT is therefore the end
that cannot see the peer disappear.

The peer is then silenced with an iptables OUTPUT drop on the PEER host
(not the DUT: XDP sits ahead of netfilter, so a local rule would never
see these packets).

--- steady state, gate armed ---------------------------------------
state=Up demand={'on': True, 'peer': False, 'tx_held': False,
                 'detect_held': True}
polls=7 polling=False rx=1 tx=195

Seven polls in eight seconds and polling=False on each sample: every Poll
was answered with a Final by stock bfdd. That is the interoperation
question settled.

--- --demand-poll-us 0 ---------------------------------------------
before: state=Up detect_held=True polls=0
STILL UP after 25s: polls 0 -> 0
  the session cannot notice its peer is gone
recovered

--- --demand-poll-us 1000000 (default) -----------------------------
before: state=Up detect_held=True polls=29
DOWN after 1.45s: diag=1 reason='detect timeout' polls 29 -> 31
recovered

diag 1 is correct and deliberate: the poll re-arms detection and the
missing Final expires it. The gate does not announce anything, it makes
the peer's absence measurable.
