Dead-man gate, both arms, same build, same 15s SIGSTOP window.
64-session mesh, DUT engine held in T state, measured entirely from the
peer's own counters because a stopped engine cannot answer SIGUSR1.

"carried" = the peer kept receiving from that session AND never recorded
a down event for it. "dropped" = the peer declared it down. The
distinction matters: a gate that answers for its first second still moves
the peer's input counter, so "received something" would read a dropped
session as a carried one.
